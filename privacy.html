Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the CheckMate Chess TV application (the "Application") handles data. The Application is a private automation tool operated by the owner of the CheckMate Chess TV channel ("we", "us") to upload our own chess puzzle videos to our own social media accounts, including TikTok.

1. Whose data we access

The Application only ever connects to social media accounts that we own and have personally authorized. It does not collect data from viewers, end users, or any third parties, and it is not offered as a service to anyone else.

2. Information accessed through TikTok

When we authorize the Application with TikTok, it uses the following scopes:

  • video.upload — to upload our own chess videos to our own TikTok account as drafts for us to review and publish.
  • video.list — to read the list of our own videos in order to confirm successful uploads and avoid duplicates.
  • user.info.basic — basic profile information (open id, display name, avatar) of our own authorizing account.

This information relates only to our own account. We do not access private messages, follower data, or any other users' information.

3. How we store and use tokens

TikTok grants the Application an access token and refresh token. These are stored locally on our own computer, are never shared with any third party, and are used only to upload our own content. They can be revoked at any time from the TikTok account settings.

4. What we do not do

We do not sell, rent, or share any data. We do not use the data for advertising or profiling. We do not combine it with data from other sources.

5. Data retention

Access tokens are retained only for as long as the Application is in use and are deleted when authorization is revoked. Upload records (video ids and dates) are kept locally to prevent duplicate posts.

6. Third-party platforms

Content uploaded through the Application is subject to the privacy policies of the destination platforms, such as TikTok's Privacy Policy.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version will always be available at this URL, with the "Last updated" date revised.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: checkmatechessyoutube@gmail.com
